About Us

A family business, one coach at a time

Our History

Sisara Coach started with a single vehicle and a simple idea: give every hire our complete attention instead of spreading a fleet thin. That approach hasn't changed.

Our Mission

To make private coach travel in Sri Lanka dependable — clear pricing, real availability, and a driver who treats your trip like his own family's.

Our Experience

Years of coastal routes, hill-country transfers, pilgrimage convoys and long wedding days have shaped how we plan every journey, down to rest stops and pickup timing.
